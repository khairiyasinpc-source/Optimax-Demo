# OPTIMAX Colourful Homepage (2026 Concept)

An Apple-inspired, colourful, responsive concept homepage for OPTIMAX Eye Specialist Malaysia.

## Open and edit

- Open `index.html` directly in a browser. No packages, API keys or build step.
- Edit content in `index.html`, colours/layout in `style.css`, interactive treatment finder in `script.js`.
- Font families: Bricolage Grotesque (headings), Instrument Sans (body), delivered by Google Fonts. Network access is needed to load the fonts; Arial fallback works offline.
- The hero photograph points to an official OPTIMAX website image. If unavailable, an in-page design placeholder appears.
- All three doctor portraits deliberately use placeholders until you provide approved photos.
- Awards/panel partner/media typography are temporary text placeholders, not reproduced logos.

## Functional areas

- Mobile-responsive navigation and 13 requested homepage sections.
- Interactive five-choice treatment finder. Guidance is informational, not a medical diagnosis.
- Accessible focus states, semantic regions, reduced-motion handling, no external JS dependencies.
- HTML-form validation, 25 branch choices grouped by region, five services plus an undecided option.
- Form confirmation reads **Request received** and clearly says this is a demo. No contact data goes to a server, is stored, or is sent to OPTIMAX. The success panel offers WhatsApp enquiry with the selected branch and service only.

## Factual and publishing safeguards

- The source pages use approximately 8 seconds per eye as a supplied marketing message. OPTIMAX's official SMILE PRO material confirms **under 10 seconds**, not a guaranteed 8 seconds for every patient. Keep the footnote and obtain medical/marketing approval before publishing.
- The 4.7 Google rating and 916 reviews were visible on the OPTIMAX homepage when checked on 14 October 2026. These figures may change.
- Branch options match the official Cambodia/branch list: 12 central, 3 northern, 7 southern, 2 east Malaysia and 1 Cambodia.
- The ISO 9001 / MHTC trust pill carries an explicit verification-pending label. Request confirmation of exact certification and current membership before replacing that label.
- Three short review quotations and names were transcribed from the OPTIMAX official homepage's embedded Google reviews. Verify permission, freshness and exact wording for production.
- The 25 centres and 31+ years figures are from the current OPTIMAX homepage. Insurance panel acceptance varies by clinic, insurer and service.
- This is a concept demo, **not** a real booking system. Connect a secure, privacy-compliant endpoint after IT approves the workflow. Do not remove the demo notice until the endpoint is functional and tested.
- It does not replace the official OPTIMAX website or the OPTIMAX Studio V2 editor.
